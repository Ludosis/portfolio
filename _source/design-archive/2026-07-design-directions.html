Why it fits

Most artistic and hand-made — the riso misregistration is a genuine printmaking gesture. Warmest personality of the three. Tradeoff: leans art-school; the technical half of the identity carries less visual weight.

A note on blending: the strongest hybrid is Drafting Table chrome with Studio Dark's lightbox treatment for project media — light, annotated pages where each game capture sits in a recessed dark frame so imagery pops without committing the whole site to dark.

All three keep the current URL structure, root-relative paths, and every word of existing content. The choice here is chrome, not content.
